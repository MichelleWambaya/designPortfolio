Kuhusu mimi — about me

Michelle

Pictures over paragraphs. Here is the short version.

Pictures first,
words second.

I'm a graphic designer in Nairobi, working mainly with organizations — NGOs, community groups, schools and events.

My niche is posters, print design and social media. Lately I've been designing more magazines and reports — long documents that still need to feel alive on every page. Brand design is my newest experiment.

I work in Canva and Affinity, and I'm moving into the Adobe suite. My style: East African pattern, bold colour, real pictures, illustrations and collage — never dull, never cluttered, straight to the point.
